  <section class="market-section market-section--soft" id="onsite-model" aria-labelledby="onsite-fit"><div class="wrap"><div class="market-section__head"><div><p class="market-kicker">When on-site adds value</p><h2 id="onsite-fit">Use physical presence when <span>the environment changes the answer.</span></h2></div><p>Travel is not the service. On-site delivery is justified when local access, hardware, network position, supervision, operating windows, or legal constraints materially affect the technical work.</p></div>
    <div class="market-card-grid"><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Sensitive networks</span><h3>Access cannot or should not be exposed remotely.</h3><p>Air-gapped, restricted, production-adjacent, or tightly controlled networks where testing must occur from an approved local position.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Hardware &amp; appliances</span><h3>The physical system is part of the attack surface.</h3><p>Devices, appliances, consoles, local services, physical interfaces, or operational workflows that cannot be reproduced remotely.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Supervised testing</span><h3>Client oversight is part of the safety model.</h3><p>Designated escorts, control-room coordination, maintenance windows, or direct technical supervision can be built into the rules of engagement.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Residency &amp; handling</span><h3>Evidence must remain inside an approved geography or environment.</h3><p>Collection, storage, analysis, and transfer can be shaped around approved client requirements where operationally feasible.</p></article></div>
  </div></section>

  <section class="market-section market-section--grid" aria-labelledby="onsite-safety"><div class="wrap market-split"><div class="market-message"><p class="market-kicker">Safety model</p><h2 id="onsite-safety">Know who can act, what can be touched, and <span>when the work stops.</span></h2><p>On-site execution is governed by the same institutional standard as remote work, with additional facility, supervision, and production-safety controls made explicit before testing begins.</p></div>
    <div class="market-visual"><span class="market-visual__title">Access and safety controls</span><div class="market-stack"><div class="market-stack__band"><strong>Authority</strong><span>Approved targets, facilities, test windows, access methods, and prohibited systems.</span></div><div class="market-stack__band"><strong>Supervision</strong><span>Client escort, technical point of contact, governance authority, and escalation ownership.</span></div><div class="market-stack__band"><strong>Stop conditions</strong><span>Operational impact, unexpected behavior, safety threshold, or scope ambiguity pauses activity.</span></div><div class="market-stack__band"><strong>Recovery</strong><span>Known rollback, restoration, and communication paths for work near sensitive operations.</span></div></div><div class="market-outcome"><strong>Bounded work remains bounded even when the environment is complex.</strong></div></div>
  </div></section>

  <section class="market-section" aria-labelledby="onsite-evidence"><div class="wrap"><div class="market-section__head"><div><p class="market-kicker">Evidence and communication</p><h2 id="onsite-evidence">Capture what proves the result. <span>Protect everything else.</span></h2></div><p>Physical access can expose more sensitive context than a remote engagement. Evidence collection therefore stays purpose-limited and client-approved.</p></div>
    <div class="market-card-grid market-card-grid--3"><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Evidence handling</span><h3>Collect the minimum required to support material conclusions.</h3><p>Photos, video, screen captures, exports, and other sensitive artifacts are taken only where explicitly permitted and transferred through approved channels.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Communication</span><h3>Keep technical and governance owners aligned.</h3><p>Cadence, escalation, unexpected impact, material findings, scope questions, and retest needs remain visible throughout the work.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Cross-border constraints</span><h3>Operate to approved requirements, not assumptions.</h3><p>Client counsel or compliance defines legal requirements. Black Bag aligns execution and evidence handling to the approved constraints.</p></article></div>
