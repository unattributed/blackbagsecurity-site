    <div class="wrap market-split"><div class="market-message"><p class="market-kicker">Evidence-first</p><h2 id="evidence-standard">A lead is not automatically <span>a finding.</span></h2><p>Scanner output, an AI suggestion, unusual behavior, or a promising attack path starts an investigation. Material conclusions require a practical standard of proof.</p><div class="market-quote">Automation can increase the amount of work performed. It does not lower the standard of proof.</div></div>
      <div class="market-visual"><span class="market-visual__title">Finding evidence standard</span><div class="market-card-grid market-card-grid--2">
        <article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Reproducible</span><h3>The path can be followed again.</h3><p>Important conditions, state, requests, responses, or artifacts are preserved where practical.</p></article>
        <article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Traceable</span><h3>The artifact supports the claim.</h3><p>Evidence remains connected to target, objective, conditions, finding, and disposition.</p></article>
        <article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Integrity checked</span><h3>The evidence remains reviewable.</h3><p>Manifests, hashes, controlled transfer, and preservation controls are used where warranted.</p></article>
        <article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Minimized &amp; protected</span><h3>Only what is needed crosses the boundary.</h3><p>Credentials, live sessions, and unnecessary client material are kept out of delivery.</p></article>
      </div><div class="market-outcome"><strong>Evidence supports the conclusion, not the other way around.</strong></div></div>
