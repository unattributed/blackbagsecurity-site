          <article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">◇</div><strong>Rules</strong><p>Permitted actions, prohibited actions, and operational constraints.</p></article>

        <p class="v24-kicker">Evidence first</p>
        <h2 class="v24-title" id="evidence-title">A signal is not <span class="v24-accent">a finding.</span></h2>
        <p>Scanner output, AI suggestions, anomalies, and promising attack paths begin investigation. Material findings require reproducible evidence that survives scrutiny.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="v24-canvas" aria-label="Signals become findings only after reproduction, challenge, and evidence">
        <span class="v24-canvas__label">Evidence earns the conclusion</span>
        <div class="v24-process">

          <div class="v24-process-step"><div class="v24-process-step__icon">◎</div><strong>Reproduce</strong><span>Make the condition repeatable.</span></div>
          <div class="v24-process-step"><div class="v24-process-step__icon">?</div><strong>Challenge</strong><span>Test competing explanations.</span></div>
          <div class="v24-process-step"><div class="v24-process-step__icon">□</div><strong>Preserve</strong><span>Capture proof and context.</span></div>
          <div class="v24-process-step"><div class="v24-process-step__icon">◇</div><strong>Qualify</strong><span>Bound the claim to the evidence.</span></div>
          <div class="v24-process-step"><div class="v24-process-step__icon">✓</div><strong>Finding</strong><span>Defensible and actionable.</span></div>
        </div>

  <section class="v24-section" aria-labelledby="retest-title">
    <div class="wrap v24-section__grid">
      <div class="v24-copy">
        <p class="v24-kicker">Verified closure</p>
        <h2 class="v24-title" id="retest-title">A fix is not proven until the <span class="v24-accent">failure path changes.</span></h2>
        <p>Retest returns to the original security condition and records what the corrected system actually does, not what the remediation intended to do.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="v24-canvas" aria-label="Original security failure path is replayed after remediation">
        <span class="v24-canvas__label">Replay the original condition</span>
        <div class="v24-linked-grid v24-linked-grid--3">
          <article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">×</div><strong>Original failure</strong><p>Preserve the condition, inputs, evidence, and impact that established the finding.</p></article>
          <article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">⚒</div><strong>Remediation</strong><p>Change the control, implementation, configuration, or architecture.</p></article>
          <article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">✓</div><strong>Verified disposition</strong><p>Resolved, mitigated, not resolved, or unable to verify.</p></article>
