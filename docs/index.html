The engagement is an evidence chain

From authorization to verified remediation.

A penetration test is useful only when another engineer can understand what happened, reproduce the material finding, make the correction, and know whether the correction worked.

What survives review

The report is not the evidence.

Reports summarize decisions. A material finding should carry its own claim, reproduction path, preserved artifacts, remediation state, and retest result. That is what lets the work survive handoff.
