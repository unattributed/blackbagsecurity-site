Black Bag Method

A finding is the end of a proof chain.

Serious testing is not the production of alerts. The method keeps each conclusion traceable to authority, observed behavior, technical proof, evidence, impact, and closure.

Authority
What is permitted?

Objectives, scope, credentials, limits, safety conditions, escalation, and prohibited actions.

Execution bounded
Observation
What happened?

Tool output and unexpected behavior remain signals until the condition can be understood and challenged.

Candidate
Proof
What does the evidence establish?

Replay, negative controls, affected path, impact, limitations, and reproducible evidence support promotion to a finding.

Finding
Closure
Did the failure condition change?

Retest starts from the original proof and records the current result instead of assuming remediation worked.

Verified outcome
