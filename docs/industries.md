<section class="section">

    <div class="cap-grid">
      <article class="cap-card reveal delay-1" data-reveal>
        <h3>Regulated and sensitive systems</h3>
        <p>Environments where access, evidence handling, data exposure, auditability, and change control require explicit engineering discipline.</p>
        <span class="cap-tag">evidence-aware</span>
      </article>
      <article class="cap-card reveal delay-2" data-reveal>
        <h3>High-availability environments</h3>
        <p>Systems where testing must respect uptime, safety, transaction integrity, or tightly controlled operational windows.</p>
        <span class="cap-tag">bounded testing</span>
      </article>
      <article class="cap-card reveal delay-3" data-reveal>
        <h3>Identity and application platforms</h3>
        <p>Applications, APIs, roles, sessions, workflows, and trust relationships where authorization mistakes can create material attack paths.</p>
        <span class="cap-tag">attack-path focused</span>
      </article>
      <article class="cap-card reveal delay-4" data-reveal>
        <h3>Emerging AI systems</h3>
        <p>Agentic, browser-integrated, model-assisted, and data-connected workflows where new trust boundaries need controlled adversarial testing.</p>
        <span class="cap-tag">emerging risk</span>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section__head reveal" data-reveal>
      <h2>What matters more than the vertical</h2>
      <p>The strongest fit is defined by the engineering problem and the consequence of getting the answer wrong.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="split">
      <div class="stack reveal delay-1" data-reveal>
        <h3>Typical objectives</h3>
        <ul class="outcomes">
          <li>Determine whether important attack paths are actually exploitable.</li>
          <li>Validate whether preventive and detective controls hold under realistic pressure.</li>
          <li>Understand the security effect of architecture, workflow, identity, or implementation decisions.</li>
          <li>Produce evidence that can guide remediation and retest.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="stack reveal delay-2" data-reveal>
        <h3>Typical constraints</h3>
        <ul class="outcomes">
          <li>Strict authorization, change, safety, or uptime requirements.</li>
          <li>Sensitive data, credentials, or evidence that require controlled handling.</li>
          <li>Limited test accounts, incomplete prerequisites, or environment restrictions.</li>
          <li>Multiple technical and executive audiences who need different levels of detail.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
