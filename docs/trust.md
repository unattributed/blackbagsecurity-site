  <section class="market-section market-section--soft" id="trust-chain" aria-labelledby="trust-controls"><div class="wrap"><div class="market-section__head"><div><p class="market-kicker">Three trust controls</p><h2 id="trust-controls">Protect the client, the evidence, and <span>the operating environment.</span></h2></div><p>Trust is not a logo wall or a policy paragraph. It is observable control over how privileged work is authorized, performed, preserved, and delivered.</p></div>
    <div class="market-card-grid market-card-grid--3"><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Evidence integrity</span><h3>Material claims remain supportable.</h3><p>Reproduction context, preserved artifacts, manifests, hashes, and controlled transfer are used where the engagement warrants them.</p></article><article class="market-card" id="confidentiality"><span class="market-card__code">Confidential handling</span><h3>Sensitive material is minimized by design.</h3><p>Credentials, live sessions, private data, screenshots, captures, and internal working state are separated from client-facing delivery.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Operational safety</span><h3>Authority stays explicit under pressure.</h3><p>Scope, prohibited actions, stop conditions, escalation paths, production constraints, and safety limits are established before risky work proceeds.</p></article></div>
  </div></section>

  <section class="market-section market-section--grid" aria-labelledby="conf-boundary"><div class="wrap market-split"><div class="market-message"><p class="market-kicker">Confidentiality boundary</p><h2 id="conf-boundary">Only necessary material should <span>cross the boundary.</span></h2><p>Internal preservation can retain what is required to support the technical conclusion. Client delivery is a separate, sanitized surface designed to avoid accidental disclosure of credentials, live session material, unnecessary data, or private working state.</p></div>
    <div class="market-visual"><span class="market-visual__title">Engagement environment → client delivery</span><div class="market-compare"><div class="market-compare__side"><strong>Internal preservation</strong><ul><li>Working evidence and provenance</li><li>Technical state needed for validation</li><li>Controlled access and retention</li><li>Private analysis and reconstruction context</li></ul></div><div class="market-compare__side market-compare__side--good"><strong>Client-facing delivery</strong><ul><li>Necessary evidence only</li><li>Sanitized reproduction context</li><li>No live credentials or sessions</li><li>Approved transfer channel</li></ul></div></div><div class="market-outcome"><strong>Minimize disclosure without weakening proof.</strong></div></div>
  </div></section>

  <section class="market-section" aria-labelledby="secure-comms"><div class="wrap"><div class="market-section__head"><div><p class="market-kicker">Communication &amp; handling</p><h2 id="secure-comms">Use the channel appropriate to <span>the sensitivity.</span></h2></div><p>Security work often involves information that should not travel casually. Communication and transfer requirements can be aligned to the client environment before testing begins.</p></div>
    <div class="market-card-grid market-card-grid--3"><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Communication</span><h3>Email by default, stronger channels where needed.</h3><p>PGP and client-approved secure portals or transfer channels can be used for sensitive material.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Data minimization</span><h3>Collect and retain only what supports the engagement.</h3><p>Retention, deletion, transfer, and evidence requirements can be made explicit during scoping.</p></article><article class="market-card"><span class="market-card__code">Private AI boundary</span><h3>Client material is not casually sent to unmanaged third-party AI.</h3><p>Local and governed approaches are preferred where sensitive engagement information could otherwise leave the controlled environment.</p></article></div>
  </div></section>

  <section class="market-section market-section--soft" aria-labelledby="disclosure-title"><div class="wrap market-split"><div class="market-message"><p class="market-kicker">Responsible disclosure</p><h2 id="disclosure-title">Client findings remain <span>client findings.</span></h2><p>Disclosure and vendor coordination follow the authority, legal requirements, client constraints, and timelines that apply to the engagement. Client findings are not published without explicit approval.</p></div>
    <div class="market-visual"><span class="market-visual__title">Disclosure decision</span><div class="market-flow market-flow--3"><article class="market-node" data-step="01"><strong>Authority</strong><p>Who owns the finding and what has been approved?</p></article><article class="market-node" data-step="02"><strong>Obligation</strong><p>What legal, contractual, or policy requirement applies?</p></article><article class="market-node" data-step="03"><strong>Coordinate</strong><p>Use the agreed channel, timeline, and client direction.</p></article></div></div>
  </div></section>
