      <div class="v24-canvas" aria-label="Trust control domains"><span class="v24-canvas__label">Three control domains</span><div class="v24-linked-grid v24-linked-grid--3"><article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">#</div><strong>Evidence integrity</strong><p>Material claims remain supportable through preserved artifacts, provenance, and controlled transfer.</p></article><article class="v24-linked-card" id="confidentiality"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">▰</div><strong>Confidential handling</strong><p>Sensitive material is minimized by design and separated from client-facing delivery.</p></article><article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">✓</div><strong>Operational safety</strong><p>Scope, stop conditions, escalation paths, production constraints, and safety limits remain explicit.</p></article></div></div>

  <section class="v24-section" aria-labelledby="conf-boundary">
    <div class="wrap v24-section__grid">
      <div class="v24-copy"><p class="v24-kicker">Confidentiality boundary</p><h2 class="v24-title" id="conf-boundary">Only necessary material should <span class="v24-accent">cross the boundary.</span></h2><p>Internal preservation can retain what is required to support the technical conclusion. Client delivery is a separate, sanitized surface designed to avoid accidental disclosure of credentials, live session material, unnecessary data, or private working state.</p></div>
      <div class="v24-canvas" aria-label="Confidentiality boundary"><span class="v24-canvas__label">Engagement environment → client delivery</span><div class="v24-linked-grid v24-linked-grid--boundary"><article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">▤</div><strong>Internal preservation</strong><p>Working evidence · provenance · technical state · private analysis · controlled access</p></article><article class="v24-linked-card v24-linked-card--bridge"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">▽</div><strong>Sanitize</strong><p>Necessary material only.</p></article><article class="v24-linked-card"><div class="v24-linked-card__icon">↗</div><strong>Client-facing delivery</strong><p>Sanitized reproduction context · no live credentials · approved transfer channel</p></article></div><div class="v24-outcome"><strong>Minimize disclosure without weakening proof.</strong></div></div>
    </div>
  </section>
